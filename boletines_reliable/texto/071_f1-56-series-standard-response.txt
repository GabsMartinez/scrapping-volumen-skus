Product Description
Reliable Model F1 series sprinklers are standard-response
standard spray automatic fire sprinklers utilizing a 5.0 mm glass
bulb thermal element.
Pendent and horizontal sidewall sprinklers may be installed
exposed or surface mounted using escutcheons such as the
Model F1-56 Pendent Model F1-56 Upright
Reliable Models B, C, or HB (reference Technical Bulletin 204).
When installed recessed, the Model F1-56 series sprinklers
are specifically listed with and may only be installed with listed
Reliable escutcheons. Refer to the technical information on the
following pages for specific listings for recessed installations and
refer to Figures 7 and 8 for dimensional information.
When fitted with an approved water shield, these sprinklers may
considered intermediate sprinklers for use in racks, below grated
walkways, and other areas where intermediate level sprinklers are Model F1-56 Vertical Sidewall Model F1-56
