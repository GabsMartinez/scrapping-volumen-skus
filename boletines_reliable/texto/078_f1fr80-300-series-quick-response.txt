Product Description
Reliable Model F1FR80-300 series sprinklers are 300 psi
(20.7 bar) rated, quick-response standard spray automatic fire
sprinklers utilizing a sensitive 3.0 mm glass bulb thermal element.
Pendent sprinklers may be installed exposed or surface mounted
using escutcheons such as the Reliable Models B, C, or HB
(reference Technical Bulletin 204). When installed recessed,
Model F1FR80-300 series sprinklers are specifically listed
with and may only be installed with listed Reliable recessed Model F1FR80-300 Upright
escutcheons. Refer to the technical information on the following
pages for specific listings for recessed installations and refer to
Figure 3 for dimensional information.
